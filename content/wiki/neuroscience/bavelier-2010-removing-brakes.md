## Summary
**성인 brain plasticity의 제한은 분자/구조 brake (perineuronal nets, myelin, E/I balance) 때문**이라는 framework를 정립한 J Neurosci symposium review. Brake를 invasive (chABC, fluoxetine) + noninvasive (perceptual learning, action video games) interventions로 풀어 amblyopia 등 성인 condition을 회복할 수 있다는 통합 thesis. **"발달 도구가 사라진 게 아니라 잠긴 것"** 이라는 dev↔adult plasticity reuse 사고의 umbrella paper.

## Key Contributions
- "Brakes" 개념 도입 — 두 종류 (structural: PNN/myelin; functional: E/I balance toward inhibition)
- Brake-lifting interventions 종합 정리 (Table 1):
  - Invasive: chABC (Pizzorusso 2002), GABA 억제, MAG/Nogo block
  - Noninvasive: SSRI (fluoxetine), perceptual learning, video games
- Amblyopia를 model로 — animal model → human translation framework
- Neuromodulator (NE, ACh, 5-HT, DA) 역할 강조 — endogenous permissive factor

## Methods & Architecture
Symposium review (no original wet experiments). 100+ references organized along brake type × intervention type matrix. Frame conceptually 재구성: plasticity = binary (가능/불가능) → spectrum (brake 얼마나 풀려있는가).

## Results
- 성체 mouse V1에서 PNN 분해 → OD plasticity 회복 (Pizzorusso 2002 인용)
- 성체 rat fluoxetine → amblyopia 회복 (Maya Vetencourt 2008)
- Human amblyopic 환자 action video game → 시각 회복 (Li/Bavelier)
- GABA precursor 이식 → second sensitive period (Southwell 2010)

## Limitations
- Animal model intervention의 human translation 안전성 미평가
- Brake-lifting 부작용 (maladaptive plasticity, 회로 destabilization) 미검증
- Action video game 같은 noninvasive intervention의 분자 mechanism 미규명
