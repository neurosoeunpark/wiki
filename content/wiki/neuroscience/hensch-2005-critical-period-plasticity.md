## Summary
Critical period plasticity의 mechanistic foundation review (NRN). V1 visual cortex에서 critical period가 **late-developing GABAergic inhibitory circuit (특히 PV+ interneuron) maturation**으로 trigger되고, **ECM proteolytic reorganization (PNN 형성)** 으로 closure된다는 통합 frame. **Critical period = active, regulated, reversible process** 라는 paradigm shift의 정초 paper.

## Key Contributions
- Critical period 정의의 mechanistic 재구성 (passive maturation → active regulation)
- **PV+ interneuron이 critical period master regulator**: GAD65 KO에서 critical period 안 열림, benzodiazepine으로 인위 trigger 가능
- **GABA maturation cascade**: Otx2 (Cajal-Retzius cell secreted) → PV+ interneuron 성숙 → perisomatic inhibition 강화 → critical period opening
- **Closing brake 분자 정리**:
  - Structural: PNN (CSPG)
  - Molecular: NgR/PirB (myelin), Lynx1 (endogenous nicotinic AChR antagonist)
- **Reactivation 전략 종합**: chABC, MAG/Nogo block, environmental enrichment

## Methods & Architecture
Comprehensive review (200+ refs). Organized by:
1. Critical period의 cellular substrate
2. GABAergic maturation timeline + TF cascade
3. ECM brake formation
4. Reactivation interventions
5. Cross-cortical area generalization

## Results
- GAD65 KO mouse: critical period 안 열림. Benzodiazepine으로 인위 opening
- Pizzorusso 2002 결과 통합 (chABC 성체 plasticity 회복)
- NgR / PirB KO: 성체 V1에서 critical period 연장 — myelin brake 확정
- Lynx1 KO: 성체 plasticity 보존 — endogenous brake 발견
- Otx2 → PV+ maturation cascade 정립

## Limitations
- 다른 cortical area (PFC, association) 의 mechanism은 V1만큼 잘 모름
- Human에서 직접 측정 불가능
- Adult reopening intervention의 specificity (원하는 회로만 열기) 보장 어려움
